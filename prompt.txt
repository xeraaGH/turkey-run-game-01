Build entry #1 for the make.fun bounty "Make the best turkey escape game" (slug turkey-run-game-01, $250, top 20 paid). Deadline Sat 29 Nov 2026 09:00 WIB; round 1 cutoff TODAY Fri 27 Nov 21:00 WIB.

GAME TO BUILD — original stealth/runner hybrid, name it "Gobble & Gone: The Great Thanksgiving Breakout" (or a better original name you choose; never copy any existing game's name). You are Tilly the turkey, escaping becoming Thanksgiving dinner. Three escalating stages in one run: (1) Farmyard — fences, haystacks to hide in, corn to gobble; (2) Barn — crates, pitchforks, narrower paths; (3) Kitchen — the oven with its open door as a heat hazard and the carving knife swinging/slicing. The farmer patrols with a vision cone: if he spots you he chases (exclamation, faster, "SPOTTED!" feedback), searches your last-seen spot, and can corner you; lose a life if he grabs you. You have 3 lives. Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to move, Space to jump, H or Shift to hide in a haystack (only while still), E to gobble corn. Pickups: corn (+score), golden feather (bonus), pie slice (speed burst). HUD shows score, distance, lives (3 feathers/hearts), stage name, and a spotted alert. End screens: CAUGHT (farmer holds the turkey, comic line, final score, restart) and ESCAPED (turkey over the fence into the woods, final score, restart). Title screen shows the game's name, the turkey, the farmer, and "Press Enter / Click to Play". Sound via WebAudio synthesis only (gobble, footsteps, alarm sting, victory jingle); M toggles mute. All art drawn in canvas code — no external assets, no network requests, no eval/new Function, no cookies/service worker. Menus work with keyboard AND mouse clicks.

TECH: vanilla canvas 2D, no library. Split source into files with one clear job each, named for what they do, under game/src/: main.js (boot, game loop, state machine: title/play/caught/escaped), turkey.js (player movement, jump, hide, lives), farmer.js (patrol, vision cone, chase, search, cornering), stages.js (farmyard/barn/kitchen layouts, obstacles, pickups), hazards.js (oven heat zone, carving knife), audio.js (WebAudio synth sounds + jingle), hud.js (score/distance/lives/stage/alert rendering), sprites.js (all canvas-drawn art: turkey, farmer, farm, kitchen, autumn palette). game/index.html loads them as classic scripts in dependency order. Keep every source file readable and complete; the judge reads file/function names as the structure signal.

SCREENSHOTS (they decide most of the verdict — make each readable at a glance when shrunk: big turkey, farmer visible, legible HUD, autumn palette): serve game/ locally and capture 4 real renders via URL query params the game reads (?shot=start / ?shot=play / ?shot=kitchen / ?shot=end, fixed seed=1): 1-start.jpg = title screen (cover, first by name), 2-play.jpg = mid-run farmyard with HUD + farmer chasing, 3-kitchen.jpg = kitchen stage with oven + carving knife, 4-end.jpg = ESCAPED end screen. Each 1280x720, JPEG quality ~80, each at most 256 KiB, short side at least 480.
